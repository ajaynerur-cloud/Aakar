# AakarKernel 1.3.0
- Exact rim fillets (torus) on boss and hole edges; exact rounds as recipes in the app
- Polyhedron primitive and exact recipes for planar solids (shell, patterns, wedge, poly) -> exact STEP
- Mirror, trimmed-torus mass properties
- Boolean retry with different tessellations (fixes bodies with holes + coplanar faces)
- Installable bundle (`dist/aakar-kernel.installable.js`), `kernel/sync.js`, tests 08 and 09
Not included: freeform/NURBS surfaces, exact shell/sweep/loft on curved profiles, general fillets on curved bodies.
