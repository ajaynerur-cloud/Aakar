# Aakar 8.7 – Part+ (advanced solid modelling)

A **Part+** ribbon tab, all as history features, with worked examples under *Examples* (Housing, Frame, Screw & spring).

- **Shape:** Draft (outer / inner / selected faces, neutral plane, two-way) with a **draft analysis** colour map; **Rib** from an open sketch line; **Fillet+** variable-radius / setback rounds and chamfers; **Wrap** (emboss / engrave on a flat face or round a cylinder).
- **Threads & coils:** ISO metric **Thread** (cosmetic or modelled helical groove), **Helix** guide curve, **Coil** (spring) solid.
- **Mould:** **Split** a solid with a plane; **Core & cavity** with shrinkage and open-mould distance.
- **Patterns:** **Along curve** (sketch curve or helix); **Mirror features**.
- **Check:** **Draft analysis** and **Wall thickness** (ray-cast map, minimum limit, histogram).
- **Weldment:** members along sketch lines (tubes, angle, channel, I-beam, bars) with mitred corners and a **cut list** (CSV).
- **Configure:** parameters × configurations (design table, CSV import / export, per-configuration suppression).

Limits: setback is a run-out at the ends of an edge; solids are faceted; wrap uses sketch outlines; weldment profiles have sharp corners.
